# Reports

The written reports are the formal deliverables. The repository also documents how to
reproduce them; the two PDFs below are the reading copies.

| Report | Period | PDF |
|---|---|---|
| Final report | November 2026 | [main.pdf](final_2026-11/main.pdf) |
| Mid-semester report | October 2026 | [main.pdf](midsem_2026-10/main.pdf) |

- The **final report** is the complete study: the controller comparison, the
  stator-resistance robustness work, and the winder results.
- The **mid-semester report** is the earlier checkpoint covering the controller
  comparison and the first robustness studies.

Each report folder keeps its LaTeX source and figures next to the PDF, so the document
can be rebuilt in place.
